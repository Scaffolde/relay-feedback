# Scaffolde Relay feedback

This is the public place to report bugs, share feedback and ask questions about
**Scaffolde Relay** (the desktop, web and iOS app) and **Scaffolde Connect**.
The Relay source lives in a private repository, so issues go here instead.

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Scaffolde/relay-feedback/issues/new/choose)

## Before you post: this repository is public

Everything you write here, including screenshots and logs, can be read by anyone
on the internet. Please do not include:

- API keys, tokens, passwords, pairing codes or QR codes, or Connect links.
- Your email address, phone number, or other people's personal details.
- Private source code, customer data, or the contents of your chats with an agent.
- Full paths that show your username (write `~/…` instead of `/Users/you/…`).
- Unredacted screenshots of your desktop, terminal, or Settings → Connections.

If a report can only be explained with private details, open the issue with what
is safe to share and say so. We will follow up privately. You can also email
pai@scaffolde.ai.

## What to choose

- **Bug report**: something is broken, crashes, or behaves differently than it should.
- **Feedback**: something is confusing, missing, or could be better.
- **Question**: you are not sure how something works.
- **Triage report**: filed by `t3 triage`, where a coding agent investigated your
  machine and drafted the report for you. It shows you the full text before it
  posts anything.

## Where to find your version

- Desktop: **Settings → About** in Scaffolde Relay.
- iOS: **Settings → About** in the Relay app.
- Web: the version shown at the bottom of **Settings** on app.scaffolde.ai.

Thank you for testing Relay.
